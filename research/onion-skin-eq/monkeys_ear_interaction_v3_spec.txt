Monkey's Ear Onion-Skin EQ — Interaction Model V3

Core principle:
Overlap is not an error. Competition is contextual and temporal.

Per-band state:
1. Occupancy: audible energy in a Bark-like critical band.
2. Overlap: minimum occupancy of two sources.
3. Level similarity: whether both are strong enough to plausibly interfere.
4. Collision episodes: contiguous intervals of meaningful overlap.
5. Longest collision duration: differentiates transient coexistence from sustained competition.
6. Repetition: recurring collisions are tracked but cannot alone escalate to masking.
7. Asymmetry: measures whether one source consistently dominates the shared band.
8. Direction: identifies which source tends to dominate.
9. Severity: band peak weighted primarily by continuity, then asymmetry, with weak repetition contribution.

Advisory classes:
- Coexistence: shared spectrum, no meaningful conflict.
- Pressure: noticeable interaction, typically brief.
- Competition: sustained or moderately strong conflict worth inspecting.
- Masking: strong, sustained interaction likely to obscure one source.

UI implication:
The onion skin should show collision episodes, not just accumulated spectral heat.
Short events appear as narrow ghost flashes.
Persistent competition accumulates a stable warning skin.
Warnings remain advisory.
Automatic EQ moves must be opt-in, bounded, recallable, and reversible.
