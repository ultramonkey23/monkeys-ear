# V4 — Envelope, harmonic and intent research

The envelope experiment separates shared energy from opposing motion. It is not a masking classifier. A stable bass can coexist with a kick; a bass that yields has complementary motion; a bass that follows the kick has shared motion. None alone proves audible masking.

Harmonic relatedness is a separate descriptor, not a discount or exemption. Estimate fundamentals only when confidence is sufficient; retain unknown for noise/polyphony. Compare partial relationships without assuming harmonic sounds cannot mask.

Preserve / Yield / Equal is user intent, not acoustic evidence. Preserve protects selected source characteristics; Yield permits bounded correction; Equal grants neither source priority. Never change warning evidence to satisfy intent.

Next: real stems, calibrated level references, auditory masking literature, listening labels, and causal streaming tests. Keep advisory warnings separate from automatic EQ. Do not integrate provisional thresholds into production.
